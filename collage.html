Fit Multiple Photos on One Printable Page

This tool takes any number of photos and automatically works out the best grid to fit them all on a single page — no manual resizing, dragging, or aligning. Just add your photos and the layout is built for you.

It's built for printing: save paper by fitting several photos on one sheet instead of printing each one separately, or build a quick contact sheet to review a batch of images at a glance.

How It Works

  1. Add your photos — drag and drop several images at once, or select them together from your files.
  2. Remove any photo you don't need by clicking the × on its thumbnail.
  3. Choose a paper size — A4 or US Letter.
  4. Click Arrange — every photo is automatically laid out in a grid on one page.
  5. Download or copy the result as a PDF or PNG image.

When to Use This

  • ✦ Save paper when printing — fit several photos on one sheet instead of printing them one at a time
  • ✦ Passport or ID photos — arrange multiple copies of the same photo for printing
  • ✦ Contact sheets — get a quick overview of a batch of photos on one page
  • ✦ Event or trip photos — build a simple printable summary sheet
  • ✦ School or craft projects — lay out reference images on one page before printing

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the auto-arrange work?

The tool automatically works out the best grid — rows and columns — to fit all of your photos as large as possible on a single page, based on how many you upload.

How many photos can I add?

There's no fixed limit. Add as many as you like and the grid automatically shrinks each photo to keep everything on one page.

Are my photos uploaded to a server?

No. Everything happens locally in your browser — your photos are never sent anywhere, so nothing leaves your device.

Can I choose the paper size?

Yes. Switch between A4 and US Letter before arranging your photos.

Can I download the result?

Yes. Download your arranged page as a print-ready PDF or as a PNG image, or copy it straight to your clipboard.
