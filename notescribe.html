Convert Handwriting to Text — Free & Instant

Notescribe is a free online handwriting recognition tool that turns photos of handwritten notes into editable digital text. No app download, no account, no subscription — just upload and convert.

Whether you're digitizing lecture notes, a journal, a recipe, or a to-do list, Notescribe reads your handwriting and gives you clean, shareable text in seconds.

How It Works

  1. Upload a photo of your handwritten notes — drag and drop or browse your files.
  2. Choose a language from our 27 supported languages.
  3. Pick an output format — plain text, bullet points, Markdown, or an interactive to-do list.
  4. Convert and instantly get editable digital text you can copy, download, or send via WhatsApp.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Notescribe completely free?

Yes. Notescribe is completely free to use with no account, no subscription, and no limits on the number of conversions.

What languages does it support?

Notescribe supports 27 languages including English, French, German, Spanish, Portuguese, Italian, Dutch, Russian, Ukrainian, Arabic, Chinese (Simplified & Traditional), Japanese, Korean, Greek, Polish, Turkish, and more.

How accurate is the handwriting recognition?

Accuracy depends on how clearly the handwriting is written and the image quality. Printed or neat handwriting typically converts with high accuracy. The output is always editable so you can fix any mistakes.

Is my handwriting data private?

Your images are processed instantly to extract the text and are not stored on our servers. No personal data is collected or retained.

What image formats are supported?

Notescribe accepts JPG, PNG, and WEBP image files. For best results, use a well-lit, high-resolution photo of your handwritten notes.
