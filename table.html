Extract Tables from Photos Instantly

The table tool reads text from your photo and automatically detects the column structure — whether your data is separated by pipes, commas, tabs, or just consistent spacing. It renders the data as a clean, styled HTML table you can scroll, copy, or download as CSV.

Works on handwritten grids, printed data tables, screenshots, and any structured text arranged in columns.

How It Works

  1. Upload a photo of your table, spreadsheet, or structured data.
  2. Choose a separator or leave it on Auto-detect — the tool picks the best one.
  3. Click Extract Table — your data renders as a clean, styled table.
  4. Copy as CSV or download a .csv file to open in Excel or Google Sheets.

What Works Best

  • ✦ Pipe-separated tables — | Col1 | Col2 | Col3 |
  • ✦ CSV data — comma-separated values, printed or handwritten
  • ✦ Spreadsheet screenshots — tabular data with clear columns
  • ✦ Handwritten grids — neatly ruled rows and columns
  • ✦ Printed data tables — reports, forms, structured lists

Frequently Asked Questions

What column separators does the tool detect?

The tool automatically detects pipes (|), tabs, commas, and consistent multi-space gaps as column separators. It picks whichever produces the most consistent column count.

Can I download the table as a CSV?

Yes. Use the Download CSV button to save the table as a .csv file you can open directly in Excel, Google Sheets, or any spreadsheet app.

Does it work on printed tables and screenshots?

Yes. The tool extracts text from any photo — printed tables in documents, handwritten grids, screenshots of spreadsheets, or structured data in any format.

What if my table isn't detected correctly?

Try a cleaner or higher-contrast photo, or switch the separator manually using the selector. Tables with clear column separators convert most accurately.
