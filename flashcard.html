Turn Notes into Flashcards Instantly

The flashcard tool reads your notes photo and automatically detects question-and-answer pairs — whether you've written them as "Q:/A:", ended questions with a "?", or simply listed terms and definitions line by line.

Each pair becomes a 3D flip card you can tap or click to reveal the answer. Navigate with the arrows or your keyboard's arrow keys.

How It Works

  1. Upload a photo of your Q&A notes, vocabulary, or study cards.
  2. Click Create Flashcards — pairs are detected and converted automatically.
  3. Tap a card to flip it and reveal the answer.
  4. Navigate with the arrows or ← → keyboard keys. Press Space to flip.

Supported Note Formats

  • ✦ Q:/A: labels — e.g. "Q: What is gravity? A: A force..."
  • ✦ Question lines — lines ending with a "?" become questions
  • ✦ Term + definition — a short term followed by a longer definition
  • ✦ Numbered pairs — "1. Question ... 1. Answer ..." patterns
  • ✦ Consecutive lines — fallback pairing of alternating lines

Frequently Asked Questions

How should I format my notes for flashcards?

The tool looks for Q:/A: labels, question marks at the end of lines, or pairs of consecutive lines. Any of these formats work — you don't need to rewrite your notes.

Can I navigate with my keyboard?

Yes. Use the left and right arrow keys to move between cards, and press Space or Enter to flip the current card.

What if my notes aren't in Q&A format?

The tool falls back to pairing consecutive non-empty lines as question/answer. So a term on one line and its definition on the next will become a flashcard automatically.

Can I download my flashcards?

Yes. Use the Download button to save all your Q&A pairs as a plain text file you can review, edit, or import elsewhere.
