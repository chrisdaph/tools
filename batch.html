Convert Multiple Pages at Once

The batch converter lets you upload several photos of handwritten notes in one go and merges every page into a single, editable plain text document — no need to convert one image at a time and paste the results together yourself.

It's built for multi-page notes: lecture notebooks, journal entries spanning several days, or a stack of meeting-note pages photographed back to back.

How Batch Conversion Works

  1. Add multiple photos — drag and drop several images at once, or select them together from your files.
  2. Remove any page you don't need by clicking the × on its thumbnail.
  3. Select your language — 27 languages supported.
  4. Click Convert — each photo is processed in order and merged into one document.
  5. Copy, download, or share the combined result via WhatsApp.

When to Use Batch Convert

  • ✦ Multi-page lecture notes — digitize an entire notebook section in one pass
  • ✦ Journal entries — combine several handwritten diary pages into one file
  • ✦ Scanned worksheets — merge multi-page assignments or exam answers
  • ✦ Meeting notes — photograph multiple whiteboard pages and get one document
  • ✦ Research notes — consolidate pages of handwritten research into a single text file

Frequently Asked Questions

How many images can I upload at once?

There's no hard limit, but for the fastest results and to stay within the free service's fair-use limits, batches of up to about 15 images work best.

Are the pages combined in the order I add them?

Yes. Pages are processed and merged in the order they were added, each labeled by page number in the combined output.

Can I remove a photo before converting?

Yes. Click the × on any thumbnail to remove that page before clicking Convert.

Can I edit the combined output?

Yes. The output area is fully editable — click anywhere to correct mistakes or rearrange text before copying or downloading.
