Summarize Notes & Documents from Photos

The summary tool extracts all text from your photo, then automatically identifies and returns the most important sentences — compressed to your chosen length. It uses an extractive approach: real sentences from your notes, ranked by importance and returned in their original order.

Perfect for long lecture notes, meeting minutes, article pages, and any document where you need the key points fast.

How It Works

  1. Upload a photo of your notes or document — drag and drop or tap to browse.
  2. Choose your length — Brief, Standard, Detailed, or Extended.
  3. Click Summarize — the key sentences are extracted and returned in seconds.
  4. Edit, copy, or download — refine the summary before saving.

When to Use Summary Output

  • ✦ Lecture notes — get the 5 most important points from a full page
  • ✦ Meeting minutes — compress a whiteboard into key decisions
  • ✦ Book pages — extract the main argument from a chapter
  • ✦ Research notes — surface the key findings quickly
  • ✦ Study revision — condense notes before an exam
  • ✦ Articles & reports — get the gist of a printed page

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the summarization work?

The tool extracts all text from your photo, then uses an extractive algorithm to score each sentence by importance and return the highest-value sentences in their original order.

Can I control how long the summary is?

Yes. Use the Summary Length selector to choose Brief (3 sentences), Standard (5), Detailed (7), or Extended (10 sentences) before converting.

Can I edit the summary after converting?

Yes. The output area is fully editable — click anywhere to refine, rephrase, or add your own notes before copying or downloading.

What types of content summarize best?

Long-form text works best: lecture notes, meeting notes, book pages, articles, and reports. Very short notes are returned as-is since they're already concise.
