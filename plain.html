Convert Handwritten Notes to Plain Text

The plain text converter reads your handwritten notes and returns the content exactly as written — no extra formatting, no bullet symbols, no markup. What you get is clean, unformatted text you can paste anywhere: a document, email, chat, notes app, or code editor.

It's the most flexible output format. Plain text works everywhere and never breaks the formatting of the destination app.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is plain text output?

Plain text is unformatted text with no added bullet symbols, markdown syntax, or special characters. The converter returns your handwriting as-is, exactly as it reads the words on the page.

Can I edit the output after converting?

Yes. The output text area is fully editable — click anywhere to correct OCR mistakes or make changes before copying or downloading.

How accurate is the handwriting recognition?

Accuracy depends on handwriting clarity and image quality. Neat, well-lit handwriting converts with high accuracy. The output is always editable so you can fix any errors inline.

What image formats are supported?

JPG, PNG, and WEBP files are supported. For best results use a well-lit, high-resolution photo with the text clearly visible against a plain background.
