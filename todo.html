Convert Handwritten Notes to a To-Do List

The to-do list converter turns each line of your handwritten notes into a checkable task item. The result is an interactive list you can use right in the browser — tap to check items off, swipe left to delete, and click any item to edit the text in place.

Perfect for shopping lists, task lists, packing lists, and any handwritten list you want to act on digitally.

How It Works

  1. Upload a photo of your handwritten list or notes — drag and drop or tap to browse.
  2. Select your language — 27 languages supported.
  3. Click Convert — each line becomes an interactive checkbox task.
  4. Check off, edit, copy, or share — the list is fully interactive immediately.

Interactive Features

  • ✦ Tap to check — mark tasks done; completed items show a strikethrough
  • ✦ Swipe to delete — swipe a task left on mobile to remove it instantly
  • ✦ Click to edit — tap any task text to correct errors or update wording
  • ✦ Copy with checkboxes — copying preserves checkbox state in rich-text apps
  • ✦ WhatsApp share — send the list with [ ] / [x] notation to any contact
  • ✦ Download as .txt — save with [ ] / [x] notation for use in any app

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I check off items in the browser?

Yes. Each converted line becomes an interactive checkbox. Click or tap it to mark the task done — completed items show a strikethrough and an amber check mark.

How do I delete an item?

On mobile, swipe the item left to delete it. On desktop, hover over the item and click the trash icon that appears on the right side of the row.

What does "Copy List" actually copy?

Copying places both a plain text version (with [ ] / [x] markers) and an HTML version (with real checkboxes) on your clipboard. Pasting into Google Docs or Notion preserves the interactive checkboxes.

Can I share the list via WhatsApp?

Yes. The "Send via WhatsApp" button formats your list with [ ] and [x] markers and opens WhatsApp's share dialog, ready to send to any contact or group chat.
